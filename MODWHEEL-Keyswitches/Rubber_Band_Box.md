Updated: 2026-09-08

Official page: https://www.modwheel.co.nz/rubberband-box

## 1. Library facts

- 6 Kontakt patches.
- 8 round robins.
- 24-bit/96 kHz multisamples, 150 MB core library.
- Requires full Kontakt 5.6 or higher.
- Sampled from MODWHEEL's handmade Tabacalyre/Rubber Band Box.
