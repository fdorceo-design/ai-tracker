## 2. Library facts

- 8 Kontakt patches.
- Three main source families: Ice Gong, BamBonezler and BamStonezler.
- Ice Gong is tuned/chromatic material from Antarctic stalactites.
- BamBonezler combines bones, seed pods, wooden frog and bamboo.
- BamStonezler combines slate, shells and scraped stone; rhythmic material is BPM-synchronised.
- Requires full Kontakt 5.3.1 or higher.
