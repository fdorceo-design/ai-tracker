# MODWHEEL Humdrum — Reference

Updated: 2026-09-08

Source policy: current official MODWHEEL product page. Exact MIDI note numbers are not guessed when the official page does not publish them.

Official page: https://www.modwheel.co.nz/humdrum

## 1. Library facts

- 12 Kontakt patches.
- Bass drums, snares and toms in large/medium/small sizes.
- Up to 9 round robins and 6 velocity layers.
- 3,200 samples, 24-bit/96 kHz; 4.0 GB core library (2.1 GB compressed).
- Requires full Kontakt 5.3.1 or higher.

## 2. Performance / control reference

| Control / feature | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Velocity | Up to 6 dynamic/velocity layers depending on patch. |
| Round robin | Up to 9 round robins. |

## 3. Notes

- Percussion mapping is patch-specific; no global articulation KS map is published.
