# MODWHEEL Timphonia — Reference

Updated: 2026-09-08

Source policy: current official MODWHEEL product page. Exact MIDI note numbers are not guessed when the official page does not publish them.

Official page: https://www.modwheel.co.nz/timphonia

## 1. Library facts

- Three folders: Regular, Irregular and ShapedNoise.
- Regular: 11 Kontakt patches + 2 Multis; hits, flams, ruffs and rolls with mallets/sticks.
- Irregular: 18 extended-technique patches.
- ShapedNoise: 23 sound-design patches.
- 24-bit/96 kHz; 7.1 GB (3.5 GB compressed).
- Requires full Kontakt 5.5 or higher.
