# BUNDLE BANSURI — HAVE Audio AI Tracker Reference

Updated: 2026-09-08

- Instrument/type: **Bansuri / Hybrid**
- Official source: https://haveaudio.com/products/bansuri

## Articulations / trigger groups

- Phrases: 6 keyswitch sound banks grouped by recorded phrase-tempo sets
- Harmonic: Airy Notes
- Harmonic: Sustained Notes
- Harmonic: Eolo Notes
- Harmonic: Vibrato Air Notes
- Harmonic: Overtone Textures
- Beatbox: All kits/elements
- Beatbox: Tuned Hits #1
- Beatbox: Tuned Hits #2

## AI Tracker notes

- Phrases NKI has 6 KS banks; Harmonic has 5; Beatbox has 3. Exact MIDI KS numbers not published.

## Mapping policy

- Exact MIDI keyswitch note numbers are included only when the official source publishes them.
- Where HAVE Audio only publishes articulation names / number of keyswitches, note numbers are left unset rather than guessed.
- Mixer, reverb and other non-performance controls are omitted unless they affect which musical event is triggered.
