# CELLO WAVES — HAVE Audio AI Tracker Reference

Updated: 2026-09-08

- Instrument/type: **Cello expansion / Arpeggiando**
- Official source: https://haveaudio.com/products/cello-waves

## Articulations / trigger groups

- Major arpeggiando loops
- Minor arpeggiando loops
- Exotic chordal arpeggios

## AI Tracker notes

- Tempo-synced and time-stretchable; treat as phrase/loop trigger material rather than ordinary chromatic articulations.

## Mapping policy

- Exact MIDI keyswitch note numbers are included only when the official source publishes them.
- Where HAVE Audio only publishes articulation names / number of keyswitches, note numbers are left unset rather than guessed.
- Mixer, reverb and other non-performance controls are omitted unless they affect which musical event is triggered.
