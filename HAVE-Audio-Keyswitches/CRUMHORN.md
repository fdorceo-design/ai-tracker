# CRUMHORN — HAVE Audio AI Tracker Reference

Updated: 2026-09-08

- Instrument/type: **Tenor Crumhorn**
- Official source: https://haveaudio.com/products/crumhorn

## Playable range

- **C2–F#3**

## Articulations / trigger groups

- Sustain Non Vibrato
- Embellished Sustain Vibrato
- Sustain Vibrato
- Staccato
- Half Tone Trill
- Whole Tone Trill

## AI Tracker notes

- 6 keyswitch articulations in each of the 3 NKI files: Open Bell / Closed Bell / Onion Bell.

## Mapping policy

- Exact MIDI keyswitch note numbers are included only when the official source publishes them.
- Where HAVE Audio only publishes articulation names / number of keyswitches, note numbers are left unset rather than guessed.
- Mixer, reverb and other non-performance controls are omitted unless they affect which musical event is triggered.
