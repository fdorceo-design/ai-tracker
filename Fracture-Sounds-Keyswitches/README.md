# Fracture Sounds — AI Tracker Reference Index

Updated: 2026-09-08

Reference notes for Fracture Sounds Kontakt libraries, organized by
product series (one subfolder per series).

## Series

- [Blueprint/](Blueprint/README.md) — the free Blueprint series (27
  individual instruments; the official catalog's 28th listing is the
  `Blueprint Collection` meta-download, not a separate instrument).
- [Main_Catalog/](Main_Catalog/README.md) — standalone paid instruments
  from the Everything Bundle catalog (23 products); bundle-only wrapper
  products aren't duplicated as separate files.

## Source policy

Official Fracture Sounds product pages are the primary source. Exact
keyswitch MIDI-note numbers are recorded only when explicitly documented
by Fracture Sounds — most Blueprint instruments don't publish one, so no
number has been guessed or inferred.
